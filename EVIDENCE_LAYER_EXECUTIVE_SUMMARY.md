## WHAT IS THE EVIDENCE LAYER?

The Evidence Layer is SANA's comprehensive system for **measuring, validating, and proving that treatments actually work**. It's the backbone of how SANA matches clients to effective practitioners and continuously improves recommendations.

**Key Insight**: Unlike other platforms that match users based on credentials or popularity, SANA weights actual patient **outcomes at 40% of credibility scoring** - the largest single component.

---

## THE 5 CORE COMPONENTS

### 1. HEALTH GRAPH (Evidence-Based Knowledge Base)
- **Purpose**: Structured knowledge linking conditions to evidence-based interventions
- **Contains**: 5 evidence strength levels (Strong, Moderate, Weak, Insufficient, Conflicting)
- **Function**: Recommends interventions with proven efficacy, checks contraindications, prevents drug-herb interactions
- **Standards**: WHO/Cochrane classification system

### 2. PATIENT-REPORTED OUTCOME MEASURES (PROMs)
- **Tools**: WHO-5 (wellbeing), DASS-21 (mental health), VAS (pain), CAM Symptom Scale (holistic)
- **Timing**: Pre-session, post-session, 1-week, 1-month, 3-month, 6-month follow-ups
- **Purpose**: Real-time validation of treatment effectiveness
- **Data**: Collected at multiple timepoints to measure immediate and sustained improvement

### 3. HEALTH SCORE CALCULATOR (SANA Health Score)
- **Input**: 5 wellness domains (Physical, Emotional, Social, Cognitive, Spiritual)
- **Output**: 0-100 score, Status level, Biological age estimate, Top 3 action levers
- **Innovation**: Each lever is ranked by evidence strength and expected impact
- **Client Benefit**: Personalized, evidence-based health dashboard

### 4. PRACTITIONER MATCHING (SPRM Algorithm)
- **Evidence Weight**: 10% of total match score
- **Formula**: (evidence_based × 60) + (interventions × 8) = 0-100 points
- **Integration**: Evidence alignment is one of 5 matching factors
- **Example Scoring**:
  - No evidence-based approach = 0 points
  - Evidence-based approach = 60 points
  - Evidence-based + 5 documented interventions = 100 points

### 5. CREDIBILITY INDEX (SANA Index)
- **Overall Score**: 0-100 practitioner credibility score
- **Component Breakdown**:
  - **Outcomes (40%)** - Patient health improvements ⭐ LARGEST
  - **Credentials (20%)** - Qualifications & degrees
  - **Volume (20%)** - Number of clients & sessions treated
  - **Completeness (10%)** - Documentation quality & PROM compliance
  - **Satisfaction (10%)** - Reviews, rebooking rate, referrals

**Critical Innovation**: Outcomes component carries 40% weight - 2x higher than credentials (20%), emphasizing real results over paper credentials.

## HOW EVIDENCE IS COLLECTED

### 8-Stage Flow:
```
1. Client Health Assessment (SISM) → baseline scores & weak domains
2. Practitioner Matching (SPRM) → evidence-based recommendations  
3. Session Booked & Paid → client appointments
4. Session Notes Documented → practitioner records treatment
5. PROMs Collected → client-reported outcomes at multiple timepoints
6. Outcomes Analyzed → improvement calculated statistically
7. Index Updated → practitioner credibility score recalculated
8. Future Matching Improved → better recommendations for next clients
```

## HOW OUTCOMES ARE MEASURED (40% of Credibility)

### Outcomes Component Formula:

**Base Improvement Score** (0-50 points):
- 50% average improvement = 50 points
- 25% average improvement = 25 points
- 0% improvement = 0 points

**Sample Size Bonus** (0-20 points, logarithmic):
- Rewards larger sample sizes = higher confidence
- 10 clients = 10 points
- 100 clients = 15 points
- 1000 clients = 20 points

**Consistency Bonus** (0-15 points):
- Rewards reliable results (low variance)
- High consistency = 15 points
- Low consistency = 0 points

**Retention Rate** (0-15 points):
- % of clients who complete treatment
- 100% completion = 15 points
- 50% completion = 7.5 points
- 0% completion = 0 points

### Example Calculation:
```
Practitioner treating 50 clients with 30% average improvement:

Improvement score:    30 points (0-50 scale)
Sample bonus:         8.5 points (log scale for 50 clients)
Consistency bonus:    12 points (reliable results)
Retention bonus:      11.25 points (75% complete treatment)
                      ─────────────
Outcomes Total:       61.75 points → 62/100

This 62/100 outcome score then:
× 0.40 (weight) = 24.8 points toward overall SANA Index
```

## HOW EVIDENCE VALIDATES TREATMENT WORKS

### Real-Time Measurement:
- **Pre-Session**: Baseline PROMs establish starting point
- **Post-Session**: Immediate effect measurement (same day)
- **1-Week Follow-up**: Short-term sustainability check
- **1-Month Follow-up**: Sustained improvement validation
- **3-Month & 6-Month**: Long-term effectiveness analysis

### Statistical Validation:
- **Mean Improvement %**: Average improvement across all clients
- **Effect Size**: Magnitude of improvement relative to baseline
- **Consistency**: Standard deviation (low = reliable results)
- **Sample Size**: Larger = higher confidence in data
- **Retention Rate**: Do clients stay? (compliance indicator)

### Safety Validation:
- **Contraindication Checking**: Automatic system prevents unsafe combinations
- **Adverse Event Tracking**: Any negative outcomes monitored
- **Drug-Herb Interactions**: Database prevents dangerous combinations
- **Population Safety**: Special protections for pregnancy, elderly, etc.

## KEY METRICS FOR INFOGRAPHIC

### SANA Index Components (Practitioner Credibility):
- **Outcomes: 40%** (Actual patient results)
- **Credentials: 20%** (Qualifications)
- **Volume: 20%** (Experience - clients & sessions)
- **Completeness: 10%** (Documentation quality)
- **Satisfaction: 10%** (Reviews & referrals)

### SPRM Matching Factors (Practitioner Selection):
- **Health Need Alignment: 40%** (Match weak domains)
- **Credibility Score: 25%** (Trust score)
- **Practical Fit: 20%** (Distance, cost, availability)
- **Evidence Alignment: 10%** (Treatment approach)
- **Preference Match: 5%** (Philosophy, style, language)

### Evidence Strength (WHO/Cochrane Standards):
- **Strong**: Multiple high-quality RCTs, meta-analyses
- **Moderate**: Some RCTs, systematic reviews
- **Weak**: Observational studies, case reports
- **Insufficient**: No quality evidence, traditional use only
- **Conflicting**: Mixed results across studies

### Health Status Levels (SANA Score):
- **0-20**: Needs Support (intervention needed)
- **21-40**: Rebuilding (active improvement phase)
- **41-60**: Balanced (stable baseline)
- **61-80**: Thriving (strong health)
- **81-100**: Radiant (exceptional wellness)

### PROMs Measurement Tools:
- **WHO-5**: 5 questions, 0-100 scale, psychological wellbeing
- **DASS-21**: 21 questions, 3 subscales (depression, anxiety, stress)
- **VAS Pain**: 1 question, 0-100 visual scale, pain intensity
- **CAM Symptom**: 6 questions, holistic symptoms specific to CAM
