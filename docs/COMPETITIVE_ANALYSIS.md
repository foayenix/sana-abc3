## Executive Summary

SANA operates in the UK complementary and alternative medicine (CAM) practitioner marketplace, which is currently **fragmented and underserved**. While directory services and booking platforms exist, **no competitor uses outcomes-based credibility scoring** to match clients with practitioners.

**Key Finding:** SANA's innovation (outcomes-weighted credibility at 40% vs credentials at 20%) positions it as the **only evidence-based practitioner marketplace** in the UK market.

---

## Market Landscape

### UK CAM Market Size
- **2023 Revenue:** USD $8.68 billion
- **2025 Projected:** USD $12-15 billion
- **2030 Projected:** USD $49.84 billion
- **CAGR:** 28.4% (2024-2030)
- **UK Global Share:** 6% of global CAM market
- **Consumer Adoption:** 40%+ of UK population has used CAM therapies
- **Annual Spending:** £543.71 million on acupuncture, chiropractic, homeopathy, herbalism, osteopathy

### UK Digital Health Market
- **2025 Market Size:** USD $15.46 billion
- **2030 Projected:** USD $36.84 billion
- **CAGR:** 18.96% (2025-2030)
- **Key Driver:** NHS digital transformation mandate, aging population (25% will be 65+ by 2043)
- **Tele-healthcare:** 57.47% revenue share (largest segment)
- **Position:** UK is Europe's leading digital health innovation hub

### Market Gaps
1. **Fragmented Discovery:** No centralized evidence-based practitioner marketplace
2. **Credibility Problem:** Practitioners struggle to prove effectiveness beyond credentials/testimonials
3. **Client Uncertainty:** No data-driven way to assess practitioner quality
4. **Booking Inefficiency:** Directories exist but lack intelligent matching
5. **Outcomes Blind Spot:** No platform measures actual treatment outcomes

## Competitive Landscape

### Category 1: Professional Directories (Low Competition)

**Competitors:**
1. **Therapy Directory (therapy-directory.org.uk)**
2. **FHT Directory (fht.org.uk)** - Federation of Holistic Therapists
3. **CNHC (cnhc.org.uk)** - Complementary & Natural Healthcare Council

**Business Model:**
- Free/freemium practitioner listings
- Location-based search
- Basic filters (specialty, location)

**Strengths:**
- Established brand recognition
- Large practitioner databases (FHT: 10,000+ members)
- Professional body backing (credibility)

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ No intelligent matching algorithms
- ❌ No outcomes tracking
- ❌ No credibility scoring beyond credentials
- ❌ No booking/payment integration
- ❌ Static listings (no quality differentiation)
- ❌ No evidence-based recommendations

**SANA Advantage:**
- ✅ AI-powered matching (5-factor scoring)
- ✅ Outcomes-weighted credibility (40%)
- ✅ Integrated booking + payments
- ✅ Real-time PROMs measurement
- ✅ Continuous system learning

### Category 2: Booking Software (Adjacent Competition)

**Competitors:**

**Strengths:**

- Recurring revenue model

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Not consumer-facing (practitioners use it, clients don't)
- ❌ No practitioner discovery/matching
- ❌ No quality scoring or outcomes tracking
- ❌ No marketplace functionality
- ❌ No evidence-based recommendations

**SANA Advantage:**
- ✅ Two-sided marketplace (practitioners + clients)
- ✅ Consumer-facing discovery platform
- ✅ Evidence layer with outcomes tracking
- ✅ Multiple revenue streams (subscriptions + commission + B2B)

### Category 4: General Healthcare Marketplaces (Indirect)

**Competitors:**
1. **Doctorly / Push Doctor** - GP telemedicine

**Weaknesses:**

- ❌ No CAM practitioner networks
- ❌ No holistic health scoring (SISM-equivalent)
- ❌ No evidence-based CAM matching

**SANA Advantage:**
- ✅ CAM-specific (complementary to, not competing with, GP services)
- ✅ Holistic health assessment (5 domains vs symptom checker)
- ✅ Evidence-based CAM matching (unaddressed market need)
- ✅ Can integrate with NHS/conventional care (referral pathways)

## Barriers to Entry

### SANA's Defensibility

1. **Proprietary Algorithms (High Barrier)**
   - SISM, SPRM, SANA Index, SCVM = intellectual property
   - 10 algorithms total with complex weighting systems
   - Difficult to reverse-engineer without domain expertise

2. **Evidence Layer (High Barrier)**
   - WHO/Cochrane-standardized health graph
   - 4 validated PROMs tools integrated
   - Contraindication database requires clinical expertise

3. **Network Effects (Medium-High Barrier)**
   - More practitioners → better matches for clients
   - More client outcomes → better credibility scoring
   - Continuous learning creates data moat

4. **First-Mover Advantage (Medium Barrier)**
   - No current outcomes-based CAM marketplace in UK
   - Brand positioning as "evidence-based" platform
   - Early practitioner/client acquisition builds network

5. **Technology Stack (Medium Barrier)**
   - Cloud-native, scalable architecture
   - Cross-platform Flutter codebase (50% faster than native)
   - Real-time capabilities (WebSocket messaging)

6. **Regulatory Readiness (Low-Medium Barrier)**
   - GDPR compliant from day one
   - Healthcare compliance-ready (NHS data standards)
   - Credential verification infrastructure

## Competitive Threats

### Threat 1: Large Platforms Entering Market
**Risk:** Amazon/Google/Microsoft launch practitioner marketplace

**Mitigation:**
- First-mover advantage + domain expertise (CAM-specific)
- Evidence layer requires clinical knowledge (not just tech)
- Professional body partnerships create distribution channel
- B2B enterprise relationships (NHS, corporate wellness)

### Threat 2: Professional Bodies Building Tech
**Risk:** FHT/CNHC build their own marketplace

**Mitigation:**
- Partner with them (data exchange, referral pathways)
- SANA provides white-label solution (B2B opportunity)
- Professional bodies lack tech expertise (10+ year digital lag)

### Threat 3: Existing Directories Adding Outcomes
**Risk:** Therapy Directory adds outcomes tracking

**Mitigation:**
- Requires complete platform rebuild (not incremental)
- Network effects: SANA's data moat grows daily
- Algorithm IP protects competitive advantage
- Speed to market critical (launch before they realize threat)
