## Innovation Assessment

### Criterion 1: Genuine Innovation

**SANA meets the innovation requirement through proprietary technology and novel business model approach.**

#### Technical Innovation

**1. Outcomes-Weighted Credibility Scoring (SANA Index)**

Unlike all competitors who rank practitioners by credentials or reviews, **SANA weights actual patient outcomes at 40%**—the highest component of practitioner credibility.

**SANA Index Formula (0-100 scale):**
```
Total Score = (Outcomes × 0.40) + (Credentials × 0.20) + (Volume × 0.20)
              + (Completeness × 0.10) + (Satisfaction × 0.10)
```

**Outcomes Component Breakdown (40 points total):**
- Improvement Score: 0-50% improvement = 0-25 points (capped at 25)
- Sample Size Bonus: 0-20 points (based on confidence from data volume)
- Consistency Bonus: 0-15 points (reliability across timeframes)
- Retention Bonus: 0-15 points (% who complete treatment)

**Innovation Proof:**
- No UK platform tracks outcomes in real-time
- No platform weights outcomes higher than credentials
- Industry standard: Credentials 80%, Reviews 20%
- **SANA disrupts:** Outcomes 40%, Credentials 20%, Reviews 10%

**2. Proprietary Algorithms (10 Total)**

| Algorithm | Purpose | Innovation |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **SISM** | Health scoring across 5 holistic domains | First CAM-specific holistic health assessment tool |
| **SPRM** | AI-powered practitioner matching | 5-factor weighted scoring (Health Need 40%, SANA Index 25%, Fit 20%, Evidence 10%, Pref 5%) |
| **SCVM** | Credential verification (99%+ accuracy) | OCR + fraud detection + 5-tier verification system |
| **SANA Index** | Outcomes-weighted credibility | Industry-first outcomes prioritization (40%) |
| **Health Graph** | Evidence-based intervention knowledge | WHO/Cochrane standardized condition-to-intervention links |
| **SOU** | Outcome Uplift (Thompson Sampling) | Reinforcement learning for continuous improvement |
| **SHAM** | Habit & Activity Model | Personalized wellness planning |
| **SST** | Safety & Triage | Crisis detection + contraindication checking |
| **SHI** | SANA Herb Index | Herbal remedy efficacy scoring |
| **SIRM** | Journal AI (8 personas) | Reflective wellness with therapeutic frameworks |

**Patent Potential:** SISM, SPRM, and SANA Index formulas

**3. Evidence Layer**

**Unique Components:**
- **Health Graph:** WHO/Cochrane evidence standards (Strong, Moderate, Weak, Insufficient, Conflicting)
- **4 Validated PROMs:**
  - WHO-5 Wellbeing Index (psychological health)
  - DASS-21 (depression, anxiety, stress)
  - VAS Pain Scale (pain intensity)
  - CAM Symptom Scale (holistic symptoms)
- **6 Measurement Timepoints:** Pre-session, post-session, 1-week, 1-month, 3-month, 6-month
- **Automatic Contraindication Checking:** Drug-herb interactions, safety flags
- **Continuous Learning:** System improves with each outcome

**Innovation Proof:**
- No CAM marketplace uses validated PROMs
- No platform measures outcomes at 6 timepoints
- No platform links evidence strength to practitioner recommendations
